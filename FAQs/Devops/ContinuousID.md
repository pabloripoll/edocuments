## Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery CI/CD
Known as CI/CD stands for continuous integration and continuous delivery/continuous deployment. In very simple terms, CI is a modern software development practice in which incremental code changes are made frequently and reliably.

In software engineering, CI/CD or CICD is the combined practices of continuous integration (CI) and (more often) continuous delivery or (less often) continuous deployment (CD).[1] They are sometimes referred to collectively as continuous development or continuous software development.[2] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CI/CD
