## Group:
A Group consists of two or more elements which are distinct in the following three ways:
· Interact over time.
· Have a sense of identity or belonging.
· Have norms that nonmembers don’t have.

## Aggregate:
An Aggregate is a collection of elements which happen to be at the same place at the same time but who have no other connection to one another.

## Category:
A Category is a collection of elements which share a particular characteristic. They do not necessarily interact with one another and have nothing else in common.
